# Controls and Gameplay

## Start and replay

1. Open `shooter.html` in a modern browser.
2. Use the **Upgrades**, **Ship Shop**, and **Profile** tabs as desired. Open **High Scores** using the personal-best card on Home.
3. Select **Launch Mission** on the home screen.
4. After losing all lives, choose **Fly Again** or return to the hangar.

## In a mission

| Action | Input |
| --- | --- |
| Move left | **A** or **←** |
| Move right | **D** or **→** |
| Shoot | Automatic |
| Use a charged ship ability | **F** or the ability button |
| Open menu / pause | **Menu** button or **Esc** |

Abilities charge by destroying asteroids. Each ship has a different kill requirement, shown in the shop and in the mission HUD. Activation consumes the full charge. Abilities vary by ship and include damage bursts, freezing rocks, repair or shielding, a dash, drones, and temporary weapon overdrive.

The ship stays near the bottom of the portrait 5:8 playfield and moves horizontally; it cannot move up or down. Asteroids fall from the top. The ability button is available in the HUD and as a touch control.

When the menu is open, choose **Continue** to resume the same run or **Quit to Hangar** to leave it. Quitting a run does not award its Star Dust.

## Touch

On phones and other touch devices, use the large on-screen **←** and **→** buttons to steer. The ship fires automatically; use the on-screen ability button when its charge is ready. During a mission, press **Menu** to pause.

## Objective and progression

Shoot incoming asteroids and avoid collisions. Rocks start small and become larger and tougher as the run continues. Spawn frequency increases exponentially to a minimum interval of 0.4 seconds, and active asteroids are capped at 18. Named celestial objects appear progressively: Solar System planets first, then stars, galaxies, and deep-space objects such as black holes and quasars. Their names refer to real astronomical objects; their gameplay scale and physics are fictional.

Standard rocks award points based on their current size tier. Rare rainbow rocks fall faster, are tougher, and award **75 points**. Destroying one grants a **5-second Rainbow Boost**: movement is 50% faster, firing is twice as fast, and the ship is invulnerable for the boost duration. The countdown is shown onscreen; destroying another rare rock refreshes it.

Each collision costs one life. A run starts with **1 life**, plus one life for each Extra Life upgrade level, and ends when all lives are lost. At the end of a completed run, earn **1 Star Dust for every 10 points**. The Zoom Engine and Rapid Blaster upgrades improve ship speed and fire rate. Hangar purchases and personal bests are saved in the current browser; scores are not submitted online.

Ships show movement speed, fire rate, shots per firing cycle, ability charge requirement, and ability description in the shop. The handling stat affects steering response but is not shown in the shop.
