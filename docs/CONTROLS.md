# Controls and Gameplay

## Start and replay

1. Open `shooter.html` in a modern browser.
2. Use the **Upgrades**, **Ship Shop**, and **High Scores** tabs as desired.
3. Select **Launch Mission** on the home screen.
4. After losing all lives, choose **Fly Again** or return to the hangar.

## In a mission

| Action | Input |
| --- | --- |
| Move left | **A** or **←** |
| Move right | **D** or **→** |
| Shoot | Hold **Space** |
| Open menu / pause | **Menu** button or **Esc** |

The ship stays near the bottom of the portrait-shaped playfield and moves horizontally; it cannot move up or down. Asteroids fall from the top.

When the menu is open, choose **Continue** to resume the same run or **Quit to Hangar** to leave it. Quitting a run does not award its Star Dust.

## Touch

On touch devices, use the on-screen left/right buttons to move and hold **Fire** to shoot. During a mission, press **Menu** to pause.

## Objective

Shoot incoming asteroids to earn **10 points per asteroid**. At the end of a completed run, earn **1 Star Dust for every 10 points**. Avoid colliding with asteroids: each collision costs one life. A run starts with **3 lives**, plus any Hull upgrade bonus, and ends when all lives are lost. Asteroids become faster and arrive more frequently as your score increases.

Spend Star Dust on upgrades and cosmetic ship skins. Hull adds starting lives, Engine increases movement speed, and Blaster increases firing speed. Your personal best and hangar purchases are saved in the current browser; scores are not submitted online.
